    Questions

    Good questions.
    Easy answers.

    Everything you need to know before you order.

    How do I order?
    +
    DM us on Instagram @nail.it.qa with your inspo photos. We'll confirm your order, discuss sizing, and handle everything from there.
    How long does it take?
    +
    Turnaround time depends on the complexity of the design. We'll give you an estimated timeframe when you place your order — simple sets are always quicker!
    Do you deliver across all of Qatar?
    +
    Yes! We deliver across Qatar. Delivery is included in all set prices. DM us for details on timings and your specific area.
    Are press-ons reusable?
    +
    Absolutely. Our gel press-on sets are made to be reapplied. Remove gently, clean, and store safely — they'll look just as good the second time around.
    Can I send my own inspo photos?
    +
    That's exactly how it works! Send us your saved inspo, a Pinterest board, TikTok screenshots — anything goes. We'll match the vibe and make it yours.
    What shapes and lengths do you offer?
    +
    We do almond, square, coffin, stiletto, oval and more. Short, medium or long — just let us know your preference when ordering.
    How do I apply them?
    +
    We include instructions with every order. You can use nail glue for a longer hold, or nail tabs for a gentler option — perfect for events.
    Do you do sets for special occasions?
    +
    Of course! Weddings, graduations, birthdays, Eid — we love a special occasion set. Order early to make sure your nails are ready in time.
