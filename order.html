Step 1: How to Size Your Nails

Accurate sizing is key to making sure your nails fit perfectly

📸 Sizing Instructions

What you'll need: A Qatari coin (any Dirham) to use as a reference for size

✓ Place the coin next to each nail to show size reference
✓ Take clear photos of sizing for BOTH hands
✓ Make sure lighting is good and nails are visible

How to photograph: Hold your hand flat, place the Qatari coin next to each finger, and take a clear photo. We'll use this to craft the perfect fit for your nails.
